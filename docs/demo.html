How this is rendered: the valve mesh is the patient's real segmented surface — its own vertices are displaced each frame using the measured commissure/opening axes to open in diastole and coapt in systole. The clip pulls leaflet vertices near its position toward the coaptation line (a physical pinch, not a decal). Blood is a particle field rendered as a continuous metaball volume, constrained by the same measured orifice / residual-EROA channel driving the metrics on the right — approximating the full FEniCSx solve that runs server-side.
